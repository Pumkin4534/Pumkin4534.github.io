03 / KNOW WHAT YOU’RE TRUSTING

NO MAGIC CLAIMS

Strong signals.
Honest boundaries.

Arc is a workflow aid. Its completion records are explicitly native-advisory, not tamper-proof attestations.

⌘

Your tools stay native.

Only MCP-owned checks receive scoped macOS Seatbelt restrictions. Other native tools and same-user processes can still modify host records.

↗

Your provider still matters.

No added telemetry service. Local task text, paths and command output are not automatically redacted. Your selected model provider receives the review context.

⊂

The scope is explicit.

Fingerprints are non-atomic and exclude ignored/generated/secret content and symlink referents. Process-group cleanup does not cover deliberately detached descendants.
